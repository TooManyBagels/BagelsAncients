# Bagels Ancients
## Content Overview
Bagel's Ancients is a mod that adds two fully fleshed out Ancients into the game.

Menitas, the Remaining Blacksmith- An act 2 Ancient who has hunted the enemies of the spire to fashion into weapons and works with upgrades.

Karyei, Unknowable Chaos- An act 3 ancient which specializes in wild effects with drastic downsides

The Ancients are intended to be balanced relatively closely with the base game, and not feel dramatically strong or weak, while providing interesting decision making.

## How to Play
To play the mod, go to the Releases and find the latest version. Unzip the folder and put it in ../Steam/steamapps/common/Slay the Spire 2/mods. If you do not have a mods folder, create one. 

Boot up Slay the Spire and you should see Bagel's Ancients in your modded settings.

## Consoling in the Ancients
If you want to experience the content without playing several runs, you can force Ancients through the console (accessible through `) with the commands:

event BAGELSMOD_MENITAS for the Act 2 Ancient

event BAGELSMOD_KARYEI for the Act 3 Ancient
